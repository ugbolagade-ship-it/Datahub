Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1.Who we are

OyoData (“we”, “us”) runs oyodata.com.ng from No 25 Sango, Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: oyodata@gmail.com.

2.Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and login. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email, name and profile picture with us.
  • Order details: what you bought, amounts, dates, references and status. This includes the phone, meter or smartcard numbers you enter for each order.
  • Wallet and referral records: top-ups, balances, referral activity and your referral code.
  • Withdrawal details: bank name and account number, only when a reseller requests a referral withdrawal.
  • Quick Buy: the email and phone number you enter.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers keep standard server logs, such as your IP address and browser type.
  • On your device: your browser stores your Quick Buy receipts and any referral code from a link you opened. This stays on your device.

We do not see or store your card number. Payments are handled by Korapay.

3.How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and log you in.
  • To process payments, deliver your orders, issue refunds and show your history.
  • To run the referral and reseller programs and pay withdrawals.
  • To give support, prevent fraud and misuse, and keep records we need by law.
  • To improve the site.

We do not sell your personal information.

4.Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with the companies that help us run OyoData:

  • Korapay to take payments.
  • EasyAccess (our supplier) to deliver orders. They receive the recipient phone, meter or smartcard number and the plan.
  • Google Firebase for login and our database, and Render and GitHub to run and host the site.
  • Google Fonts to load the site's fonts, which may share your IP address with Google.
  • The authorities or other parties where the law requires it, or to protect against fraud.

Some of these providers keep data on servers outside Nigeria.

5.How we protect it

Login is handled by Google Firebase, and all pages are served over HTTPS. Our database can only be reached through our server, which checks your login before it shows or changes your data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your login private.

6.How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as we need it to deal with disputes, prevent fraud, and meet our record-keeping duties. After that we delete it or remove what identifies you.

7.Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it (where we are not required to keep it), or stop using it for certain purposes. To do this, contact us at oyodata@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8.Children

OyoData is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9.Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies, to remember things like your Quick Buy receipts and referral code. You can clear this in your browser settings, but you may lose saved receipts.

10.Sign in with Google

If you choose Google sign-in, we use your Google email, name and profile picture only to create and sign in to your OyoData account. We do not use it for advertising and we do not share it except with the providers listed above that run the site.

11.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
